How to get help

Send an email to hello@getafterdone.com with:

  • your Atlassian site URL (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net);
  • what you expected and what happened;
  • the Jira issue key and the Confluence page link, if your question is about a specific result;
  • a screenshot, if it helps.

Please don't send passwords, API tokens, or confidential page content. We never need them.

What we support

  • Installing, configuring, and using the App on Jira Cloud and Confluence Cloud.
  • Bugs and unexpected results, including findings that look wrong.
  • Questions about licensing, data, privacy, and security.

Support is by email only. We don't offer phone support or 24/7 on-call coverage.

Security issues

To report a vulnerability, email hello@getafterdone.com with "Security" in the subject. See the Security page.

Billing and refunds

Purchases, invoices, and refunds are handled by Atlassian through the Atlassian Marketplace. Contact Atlassian for billing; we're happy to help with anything about the App itself.
